Reranking Gateway

Reorder candidates using stronger relevance judgments.

Takeaway: The Reranking Gateway demonstrates that 'retrieval' is not a single step but a pipeline. By decoupling broad candidate generation (Hybrid Retrieval) from precise relevance scoring (Reranking), we achieve the best of both worlds: the breadth of lexical/semantic search and the precision of deep contextual understanding. This architecture is the current industry standard for high-accuracy RAG systems.
